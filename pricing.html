Pricing How it works 04 / 05

What does a product shoot cost?

There isn't one number. A forty-product catalogue and a single campaign frame can cost the same and take completely different weeks. Here is what actually moves the price — so you can work out roughly where your project sits before you ask.

  • Quotes are built from the brief, not from a rate card
  • Costs are agreed in writing before the shoot
  • Anything extra is quoted separately, not added later

01Variables

Twelve things that move the number.

Most of these are decided by you, in the brief. The more precise the brief, the closer the quote is to the final invoice.

01

Number of products

Per-product effort drops as the count goes up, if the products are similar.

02

Images per product

Three angles is a different shoot from nine angles plus detail crops.

03

Photography style

Plain packshots are quick. Styled and concept work takes set-up time.

04

Product complexity

Glass, chrome, jewellery and transparent packaging all need more lighting.

05

Styling and props

Surfaces, fabrics, ingredients and set pieces are sourced and costed.

06

Models

Hand models or full models add casting, time and usage to the budget.

07

Retouching depth

Standard cleanup is included. Compositing and heavy finishing is not.

08

Video

Motion shot alongside stills costs less than motion booked on its own.

09

Creative direction

Concepting, references and art direction are separate from shoot time.

10

Turnaround

A normal schedule costs what it costs. Compressing it costs more.

11

Usage

Your own channels are straightforward. Paid print and outdoor is agreed upfront.

12

Location

Shooting outside the studio brings travel, transport and set-up time.

03Getting a quote

Send four things.

A quote takes about ten minutes to put together if the brief is clear, and a week of back-and-forth if it isn't. These four cover almost everything:

  • A product list — how many, and what they are.
  • Where the images will be used: marketplace, own site, social, print.
  • Two or three reference images you like, and one you don't.
  • The date you need the files by.

If you're not sure yet, send what you have. It's usually enough to narrow the number down to a range on the first call.
