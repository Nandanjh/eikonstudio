04Why Icon Studio

How we think about a shoot.

Four things that decide whether an image works once it leaves the studio.

01

Product first

The set exists to serve the product. If a prop is competing with what you're selling, it goes.

02

Consistency

A catalogue has to look like one catalogue — same light, same angles, same colour from the first frame to the two hundredth.

03

Commercial thinking

Every image has a job and a place. We shoot for the crop, the platform and the size it will actually be seen at.

04

Detail

Lighting, texture, colour and retouching are part of the product, not something applied at the end.

05Process

Five steps, no surprises.

01

Brief

We go through the products, the platforms and what the images need to do.

02

Plan

Shot list, references, styling and props agreed before anything is switched on.

03

Shoot

Controlled studio lighting, checked frame by frame against the list.

04

Retouch

Cleanup, colour correction and consistency across the whole set.

05

Deliver

Final files sized and named for where they are going.

06Kolkata

A studio you can walk into.

Product photography goes better when someone from the brand can see the set. Being in Kolkata means you can hand the products over in person, watch the first few frames, and fix the styling while the lights are still up — instead of describing what's wrong over email a week later.

  • Products handed over in person, or couriered if that is easier.
  • Pickup and drop-off can be arranged depending on the project.
  • Same-city coordination for approvals, additions and reshoots.
  • Local brands can review selects at the studio before retouching starts.

Simple packshots move quickly. Reflective packaging, jewellery and styled sets take longer because each one needs its own lighting. Tell us the product count and the number of angles per product and we'll give you a realistic figure before the shoot, not after it.
