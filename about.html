About Anushree, founder 03 / 05

The person behind Icon.

Icon Studio is small on purpose. The person you speak to is the person behind the camera, and the one who decides whether a frame is finished.

  • Founder & photographer — Anushree
  • Studio — Kolkata, West Bengal
  • Works with D2C, e-commerce, retail and local brands

01Why the studio exists

Good products, badly photographed.

I started Icon Studio because I kept seeing the same thing: a brand spends a year on the formula and months on the packaging, then puts up an image shot against a curtain at home. The product deserved better — and so did the person deciding whether to buy it.

Most of my work is quiet. Getting the light right. Getting the colour right. Making the fortieth frame match the first. It isn't the exciting part, but it is the part that makes a catalogue look like it belongs to one brand instead of five different afternoons.

I prefer working directly with the people who make the product. Nothing gets lost through a chain of coordinators, and decisions get made while the lights are still up.

02Her approach

Four things, in this order.

A shoot goes wrong when it starts with the camera. It goes right when it starts with the product.

01

See the product

Before anything is lit, I want to know what it's like in the hand — the weight, the finish, where the light catches and where it doesn't.

02

Understand the brand

The same bottle can be shot ten ways. Which one is right depends on who you're selling to and where they'll see it.

03

Build the frame

Surface, angle, composition and light — decided on purpose rather than found by accident and kept because the shoot ran late.

04

Make it feel right

There's a point where an image is technically correct and still flat. Closing that last gap is most of the job.

03Working with Anushree

How a project actually runs.

  • It starts with a conversation, not a quote — what the products are, where the images will live, and what isn't working about the current ones.
  • You get a plan before the shoot: shot list, styling direction, and how many final images you'll receive per product.
  • You're welcome at the studio for the first set-up. Most brands come for the first shoot at least.
  • Selects are shared before retouching starts, so nothing is finished without your sign-off.

04The standard

What makes a product image good.

It's honest about the product. It looks like the thing that arrives in the box. It reads at thumbnail size and holds up at full zoom. And it sits next to the rest of the range without anyone noticing it was shot on a different day.
